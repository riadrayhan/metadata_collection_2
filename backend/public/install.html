📲 Data Collector App

Follow the steps below to install the app on your phone

⬇️ Download APK

Step 1 — Allow installation from unknown sources

1

Open Settings

Go to your phone's Settings app.

2

Find "Install Unknown Apps"

Navigate to:

Settings → Apps → Special App Access → Install Unknown Apps

— or on some phones —

Settings → Security → Install Unknown Apps
3

Allow your browser or file manager

Tap on Chrome (or your browser / Files app) and turn "Allow from this source" ON.

then

Step 2 — Temporarily disable Play Protect

4

Open Play Store

Tap your profile icon (top-right corner).

5

Go to Play Protect

Profile → Play Protect → ⚙️ Settings
6

Turn off scanning

Toggle "Scan apps with Play Protect" to OFF. Tap Turn off to confirm.

⚠️ You can turn Play Protect back ON after installation is complete.
finally

Step 3 — Install the app

7

Open the downloaded file

Open your Downloads folder and tap DataCollector.apk.

8

Tap "Install"

Tap Install on the installation screen. Wait for it to finish.

9

Open the app

Tap Open or find Data Collector in your app drawer.

✅

All done! The app will ask for permissions and start automatically.
